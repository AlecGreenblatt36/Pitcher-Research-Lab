CURRENT DETECTION METHOD

Sustained-deviation screening

Current sustained-change detection uses a three-outing rolling average against the pitcher’s historical outing-level mean and standard deviation. Three consecutive rolling values beyond ±2 standard deviations create a sustained-change flag.

The comparison cards report every eligible baseline-to-target window. A sustained-change label appears only when three consecutive rolling values exceed the ±2 standard-deviation threshold. A detected date is an investigation point, not proof of when or why a physical change occurred.
